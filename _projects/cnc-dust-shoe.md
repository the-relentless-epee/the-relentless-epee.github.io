![CNC dust shoe](/assets/images/projects/cnc-dust-shoe.svg)

The shop vac was doing more work than the router. This dust shoe wraps the spindle with a clear window so I can still watch the cut, and the brush skirt attaches with magnets for quick bit changes.

The upper plate is bolted to the spindle mount; the lower ring lifts off without tools. A 2.5 inch hose adapter points the suction away from the gantry.

- Laser-cut acrylic window
- Replaceable nylon brush strips
- Magnetic quick-release lower ring
