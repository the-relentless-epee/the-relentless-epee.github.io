The bench had become a horizontal storage system. This cart keeps the most-used tools within arm's reach, with a folding side table for the drill press and a French cleat rail on the back.

Printed bins hold fasteners and bits, and every drawer has a label plate that pops out when the contents change.

- Birch plywood carcass with locking casters
- 3D-printed drawer bins and dividers
- Folding side table and French cleat rail
