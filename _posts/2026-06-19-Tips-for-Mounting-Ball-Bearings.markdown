When using a single bearing to support an off-axis load, there is a cantilever moment that wants to twist the bearing out of its housing. Press-fit is often not ideal, especially with 3D-printed housings. 

for thin-wall bearings that have a large enough inner diameter to fit some printed features, using low-profile screws to clamp down the bearing is very secure.

To do this in CAD, simply design screw holes around the circumference of the bearing. Make sure there is some overlap between the edge of the screw head and the bearing race so there is sufficient clamping.
