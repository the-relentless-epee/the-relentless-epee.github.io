I'm Leon, and I like to make things :) I'm currently studying Engineering Physics at UBC, and making projects in my free time.

I'm interested in all fields relating to robotics, like actuator design, linkage mechanisms, power electronics design, firmware & algorithms, and machine learning.

My engineering/making journey started back in 2020, when I got an Ender 3 for my first printer. I made many fun projects with it, and I became quite good at optimising my designs for 3D printing. Over the years I learned to use microcontrollers, various sensors, actuators, and cameras in my projects. One of the most fun projects was the one that I made pre-AI: a face-tracing nerf turret that defends my room from my sister. 

Anyways, thanks for checking out my website, hope you find some inspiration from my projects!
